The AI reads. People decide.

Firstcall puts every lead from email, web forms and call services on one rail. The AI turns each messy message into a clean ticket; a person claims it, and its colour shows how long it has waited.

Four leads on the rail

    These four leads are made-up test messages. On 500 of them, 0 went to the wrong buyer. See the proof

    Nobody owns them yet; only a person can claim one.

    Notes get the same reading

    After a call, a salesperson types a quick note. The AI tidies it and writes the buyer's budget and needs to the profile at once, never over a value a person set.

    Only the contact it suggests waits: nothing is logged until a person presses Confirm, so a wrong suggestion costs one tap.

    House rules for the AI

    It does

    • Read every message and print the ticket: the buyer, their phone and email, what they want, their agent, and the team.
    • Use fixed rules first. The model's answer counts for only two fields, the buyer's name and the kind of message, and only where the rules found nothing or weren't sure. Tests on made-up messages picked those two.
    • Tidy a salesperson's note, write the buyer's budget and needs to the profile, and suggest the contacts the note describes. A value a person set is never overwritten.

    It never

    • Emails a buyer. Email goes only to the company's own active users.
    • Logs a contact by itself. A suggested contact saves nothing until a person presses Confirm.
    • Claims a lead. A person does, and the timer shows how long it took.
    • Runs unless someone switches it on. The model is off by default, and then every message takes the rules path.

    The proof, and its limits

    500 made-up messages went through the shipped pipeline in one run, with its methods fixed before it started.

    The messages and their right answers come from the same generator, so this shows the pipeline does what it was built to do. It does not measure accuracy on real mail. Misses are published, and each decision states its prediction before the result.
