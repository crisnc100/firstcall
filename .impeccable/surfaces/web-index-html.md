# Firstcall front page

## Scope and mode
A new public front page (Persuade). The signed-in team app (board, lead detail, inbox, alerts) stays where it works today and is restyled into this world in a later PR (Operate).

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
- Hiring managers and business leaders or owners judging Cristian as an applied-AI builder and consultant.
- They must get in seconds: the AI reads messy emails and notes and tidies them; people decide and confirm; no lead waits unseen.
- Primary action: watch one messy message become a clean ticket and claim it on the page; then read the method and the proof; secondary: the code on GitHub.
- Proof: the confirm run on 500 made-up messages (every safety floor met: 0 wrong merges, 0 wrong attaches, 0 attacks that changed a field, 0 agent contacts taken as the buyer's), always labelled as made-up data. No invented customers or claims.
- Avoid: salesy, the AI-startup look, busy. Less is more. Must look clearly different from Askwell's Audited Ledger.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every lead is a printed ticket on a steel rail: the AI prints the clean ticket from a messy message, its colour stripe ages while it waits, and a person calls "heard" to claim it. It refuses the SaaS landing page (headline beside a dashboard screenshot, a row of feature cards).
OWN-WORLD: Brushed steel rail and pass as the ground; thermal ticket paper; ink near-black; age stripes run green, then heat-lamp orange, then late red, and only state uses them. A condensed, industrial sans for labels and figures, monospace for printed ticket text. Hard edges, no rounded cards, no drop shadows beyond the ticket's own paper edge.
STORY: The visitor reads the promise, sees a messy email print out as a clean ticket on the rail, watches the age stripe turn, claims it with "Heard", sees that nothing changes until a person confirms, reads what the AI does and never does, reads the proof, then visits the code.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top rule with Firstcall and three links. A one-line promise at display size and two plain sentences. Below, a full-width steel rail holding four tickets: the first, larger, shows the raw messy email above the clean fields the AI printed (buyer, phone, wants, agent, team) with a Heard — Claim key; the others age green to orange to late red with their timers. One proof line under the rail.
FORM: On the Rail, Impeccable's pick from the grounded list (restaurant ticket rails and kitchen printers), chosen by Cristian; seed:9d37311b472382c0. Raises: the hottest colour is reserved for the lead waiting longest (from Polish Film Poster); state changes are instant, a claimed ticket swaps at once with no fade (from Elbow Panel Console); a lead's messages are joined by one visible rule (from Memory Quilt).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
